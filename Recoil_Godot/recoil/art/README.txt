Drop these files from Kenney's "Space Shooter Redux" pack into this folder.
Anything missing is replaced by a simple colored shape, so nothing breaks.

Textures (PNG folder):
  playerShip1_red.png     player ship
  enemyBlack1.png         enemy ship
  laserBlue01.png         player bullet
  laserRed01.png          enemy bullet
  meteorBrown_big1.png    asteroid
  darkPurple.png          tiled background

Sounds (Bonus folder):
  sfx_laser1.ogg          player shoots
  sfx_laser2.ogg          enemy shoots
  sfx_zap.ogg             enemy hit / dies
  sfx_shieldDown.ogg      player hurt
  sfx_lose.ogg            game over

Font (Bonus folder):
